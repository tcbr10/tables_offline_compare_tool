Privacy and limitations

Your tables are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded. Paste, CSV and TSV work offline. Excel import/export downloads SheetJS from its CDN on first use and requires internet. The selected worksheet's data is retained, not styling, formulas or other sheets. Excel export stores values as text; CSV does not preserve column types and spreadsheet apps may interpret formulas. Already-rounded identifiers cannot be restored.
